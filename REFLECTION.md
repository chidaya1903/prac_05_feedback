What have you changed about your process in this subject based on your personal reflection?
I now plan each prac before coding, break work into small milestones, and commit after each milestone with clear messages. I also use consistent function/class structure (main(), SRP), and run quick tests as I go instead of waiting until the end.

What do you still need to change in terms of your approach, application, time on task, attitude, etc.?
I need to start earlier, set fixed study blocks, and stick to them. I’ll reduce context-switching, ask for help sooner when stuck, and write brief test scaffolds before polishing output.

What are you doing well in the subject so far that you will keep doing?
I’m following naming/convention guidelines, using Git properly (branches, PRs, and refactoring to keep functions and classes focused. I also document with short, useful docstrings and comments.

What do you need to stop or change in your work for this subject overall so far?
Stop leaving formatting and validation to the end. I’ll include input checks, constants, and f-string formatting from the start. I’ll also avoid large “all-in-one” commits and long unbroken coding sessions.

What is one request you have or change you suggest for this subject and/or your IT degree overall?
Provide a few more small, real-world mini-examples per week (with both procedural and OOP versions) and a short rubric snippet for each prac showing what “good” looks like for commits, structure, and tests.
